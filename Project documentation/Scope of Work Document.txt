SCOPE OF WORK
Memory & Ritual Preservation Platform
Market Test Phase, Landing Page & Payment Integration
Date: July 2026   |   Client: Nawomica   |   Developer: Sanket Raj
1. Parties Involved
This Scope of Work ("SOW") forms a binding agreement between the two parties upon signature and shall be governed in accordance with the applicable laws of India. This SOW, together with any attachments, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to the subject matter herein.
2. Project Overview
The Developer agrees to design, develop, and deploy a single-page marketing and conversion website for the Client. The purpose of this engagement is to validate real market demand for the Client's memory and ritual preservation service by attracting visitors, building trust, and converting them into paying customers or booked consultation calls prior to any investment in a full-stack platform.
The Developer shall also create a complete brand identity for the Client from scratch, including a logo, colour palette, and typography system, all of which shall be delivered to the Client upon project completion. The platform shall include ad and conversion tracking (Meta Pixel and Google Ads/GA4) and a cookie consent banner in compliance with the DPDP Act and Meta/Google platform policies.
3. Scope of Work, Features Included
4. Out of Scope
The following are explicitly NOT included in this engagement unless separately agreed upon in writing and signed by both parties:
Domain registration and ongoing web hosting subscription fees
Third-party API costs, including Razorpay gateway transaction fees and any platform subscription costs
Ritual content, the detailed family-specific content (Samagri, steps, songs, photos, relative roles) comes from each paying customer via the Google Form intake; the Client only needs to provide 9 brief ritual descriptions (2-3 sentences each) for the landing page
PDF production: The Developer is responsible for setting up the Google Form data destination pipeline. The actual manual task of opening the Canva template, copy-pasting customer-submitted form answers (including Gotra, Kuldevi, Kuldevta, and ritual lists), and exporting/emailing the final PDF file is an operational execution requirement of the Client, falling entirely outside the developer's technical engineering obligations under this contract 
Dynamic pricing tiers (Rs. 499 / Rs. 999 / Rs. 1,499) and discount code infrastructure,  deferred to Phase 2 as confirmed in the discovery worksheet
User registration, login, or any account or session management system
Personal user dashboard, ritual milestone tracking, or any dynamic user interface
Physical book printing, print-on-demand integration, or any fulfilment logistics
CRM integration (Zoho, HubSpot, Salesforce, or any equivalent)
Email marketing platform integration (Mailchimp, Klaviyo, or any equivalent)
Mobile application development (iOS or Android)
Subscription or recurring billing functionality
Multi-language or internationalisation (i18n) support
A separate live-chat widget beyond the floating WhatsApp button
Paid or premium stock photography licensing, if free licensed libraries (Unsplash/Pexels-tier) are insufficient, any subscription cost is separate and passed through directly
Ongoing maintenance beyond the 21-day post-launch bug-fix window
Any feature not explicitly listed in Section 3 of this document
Any request to include out-of-scope items shall be treated as a Change Request and shall require a separate written scope, timeline, and fee agreement before work commences.
5. Project Milestones & Acceptance Criteria
Each milestone requires written approval from the Client before the next phase begins. Approval must be provided within 2 business days of delivery. Silence beyond 2 business days shall be treated as tacit approval and the project shall proceed to the next milestone.

8. Payment Terms
Total Project Fee: Rs. 51,000  (34 hours × Rs. 1,500/hr,  fixed fee inclusive of brand identity, all features listed in Section 3, and ad tracking setup)
All payments are to be made via bank transfer, UPI, or Razorpay link within 2 business days of the milestone trigger
Late payment beyond 5 business days of the due date shall pause all project work until the outstanding amount is received
The kickoff payment (50%) is non-refundable once design work has commenced
Final payment is due before handover of source code, deployment credentials, and brand assets
9. Revision Policy
10. Termination Clause
Either party may terminate this agreement with 5 (five) days' written notice delivered via email or WhatsApp to the other party's registered contact.
If the Client terminates, all work completed to date shall be billed at the agreed rate of Rs. 1,500/hr. The kickoff payment (50%) is non-refundable once design work has commenced. The Developer shall deliver all completed work within 5 business days of the termination notice.
If the Developer terminates, all payments received beyond the value of work completed to date shall be refunded to the Client within 5 business days. The Developer shall deliver all completed work and documentation to the Client.
Termination for cause: either party may terminate immediately and without notice if the other party materially breaches this agreement and fails to cure such breach within 3 business days of written notice of the breach.
11. Intellectual Property & Confidentiality
11.1  Ownership
Upon receipt of full and final payment, all source code, design assets, brand materials, copy, and related intellectual property developed under this agreement shall be transferred to and become the sole property of the Client. Until full payment is received, all intellectual property remains the property of the Developer.
11.2  Portfolio Rights
The Developer retains the right to display the project name and non-sensitive screenshots in their professional portfolio. The Developer shall NOT share source code, business logic, proprietary processes, pricing details, or any Client data publicly without the Client's prior written consent.
11.3  Confidentiality
Both parties agree to keep all project details, pricing, technical specifications, and business information exchanged under this agreement strictly confidential during and for 2 (two) years after the completion or termination of this agreement. This obligation shall survive termination of this SOW.
12. Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)
Both parties acknowledge that during this engagement, each may have access to confidential and proprietary information belonging to the other. Both parties agree to the following terms:
12.1  Definition of Confidential Information
Confidential Information includes, but is not limited to:
Business plans, strategies, financial data, projections, and roadmaps
Technical specifications, source code, algorithms, system architecture, and database schemas
Customer data, user information, and all personally identifiable information processed by the platform
Pricing terms, vendor relationships, and all commercial arrangements under this agreement
Any information that a reasonable person would consider confidential given the nature and context of the disclosure
12.2  Obligations of Both Parties
Neither party shall disclose the other party's Confidential Information to any third party without prior written consent
Each party shall use the Confidential Information solely for the purpose of fulfilling obligations under this agreement
The Developer shall not use, replicate, or repurpose the Client's business idea, platform logic, user data, or proprietary processes for any other client or personal project, during or after this engagement
Both parties shall take reasonable measures to protect the other's Confidential Information, using no less care than they use to protect their own confidential information
12.3  Exceptions
Confidentiality obligations do not apply to information that: (a) is or becomes publicly known through no breach of this agreement by the receiving party; (b) was already in the lawful possession of the receiving party before disclosure; (c) is independently developed by the receiving party without reference to the Confidential Information; or (d) is required to be disclosed by applicable law or court order, provided the receiving party gives the disclosing party prompt written notice and cooperates with any effort to obtain a protective order.
12.4  Duration
These NDA obligations shall remain in force during the project and for 2 (two) years following termination or completion of this agreement, whichever is later.
12.5  Breach & Remedy
Both parties acknowledge that a breach of this NDA may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages alone would be an insufficient remedy. In such cases, the non-breaching party shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief, specific performance, or other equitable remedies in addition to any other remedies available under applicable law, without the requirement to post bond.

14. Dispute Resolution
14.1  Good Faith Negotiation
In the event of any dispute, claim, or controversy arising out of or relating to this agreement or its breach, the parties shall first attempt to resolve the matter through good faith negotiation. Either party may initiate this process by serving written notice of the dispute to the other party. The parties shall have 15 (fifteen) business days from the date of such notice to resolve the dispute through negotiation.
14.2  Mediation
If the dispute is not resolved through negotiation within the period specified in Clause 14.1, either party may refer the matter to mediation. The mediation shall be conducted by a mutually agreed mediator in Bangalore, Karnataka. The costs of mediation shall be shared equally between the parties unless otherwise agreed.
14.3  Arbitration
If the dispute remains unresolved after mediation, it shall be finally settled by binding arbitration in accordance with the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996 of India (as amended). The arbitration shall be conducted by a sole arbitrator mutually appointed by the parties. If the parties cannot agree on an arbitrator within 10 (ten) days, the arbitrator shall be appointed in accordance with the rules of the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.
The seat and venue of arbitration shall be Bangalore, Karnataka, India
The language of the arbitration shall be English
The arbitrator's award shall be final and binding on both parties
Each party shall bear its own legal costs unless the arbitrator orders otherwise
14.4  Continued Performance
Unless the agreement has been formally terminated in accordance with Clause 10, both parties agree to continue performing their obligations under this agreement during any dispute resolution process, to the extent reasonably practicable.

Nothing in this Clause 15 shall limit or exclude liability for: (a) death or personal injury caused by negligence; (b) fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation; (c) wilful misconduct or gross negligence; or (d) any other liability that cannot be excluded or limited under applicable Indian law.

The Client acknowledges that such services operate under their own terms and conditions.
16. Project Completion Criteria
The project is considered complete and the final payment of Rs. 25,500 is due when all of the following acceptance tests have been confirmed by both parties in writing:
The page is live and accessible at the agreed custom domain with a valid SSL certificate
All 10-page sections are visible, correctly styled, and functional on desktop, tablet, and mobile
All 9 ritual cards display correctly on the landing page with content pulled from Sanity CMS, specifically validating that Card 9 functions as a dynamic "Custom / Exceptional Family Ritual (DIY)" module that correctly accepts unique user-defined ritual fields 
The Coming Soon waitlist pop-up opens and captures an email address when a locked ritual card is clicked

The Google Form 'collect email + edit after submission' feature is active, and the Apps Script sends a resume link to the submitter's email

Meta Pixel and Google Ads/GA4 events (page-view and payment-complete) are confirmed to be firing correctly in the respective platform dashboards
The cookie consent banner displays before any tracking script fires and functions correctly across all major browsers
The Calendly embed displays and allows appointment selection
The floating WhatsApp button opens a conversation correctly on mobile
The enquiry form submits successfully and delivers a notification email to the Client
All brand assets ,  logo SVG, .png, colour tokens, and font specifications have been delivered to the Client
A Sanity Studio and Google Form handoff walkthrough call has been completed
