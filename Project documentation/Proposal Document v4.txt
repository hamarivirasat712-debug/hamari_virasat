PROJECT PROPOSAL, MARKET TEST PHASE
Memory & Ritual Preservation Platform
A conversion-focused landing page to validate real market demand before building the full platform.
Prepared by Sanket Raj  ·  Full-Stack Web Developer  ·  July 2026
What you are building, and why it matters
Most Indian families carry their rituals in memory alone. The Godbharai songs, the Mundan ceremony steps, the specific role of the Mama in a Chatti Puja- these details are not written down anywhere. They are passed from person to person, and every generation that passes without documentation makes them harder to recover.
What you are building changes that. A structured platform where families can record the rituals that define them, from Prebirth to the first ritual in a wedding, capture the Samagri, the special food, the songs, the photographs, and the precise roles of every relative, and preserve it all in a form that lasts.
Your discovery worksheet confirmed the emotional core that should drive this: the fear of losing family identity as generations pass. That "generational gap" hook now anchors both the ad creative and the page copy; everything else on the page exists to support that one feeling.
The market test phase is about proving one thing: will Indian families pay for this? Everything in this proposal is designed to answer that question as quickly and affordably as possible.
How we deliver quality
Two architectural decisions bring this under Rs. 50,000 without cutting a single feature. First, the 9 ritual cards on the landing page are static marketing content, brief descriptions the client writes once,  not 54 detailed content fields we need to enter manually. Second, the post-payment intake form is a Google Form, not a custom-coded form,  now enhanced with a save-and-resume link, per your worksheet, so customers are not forced to finish everything in one sitting.
Note on Operations vs. Development Scope: While the business model leverages a pre-designed Canva template as the smart way to bypass expensive custom automated PDF generation code, the manual assembly and delivery of these PDFs is strictly an operational business task managed by the Client. Therefore, it is marked as "Out of Scope" in the developer agreement to protect the technical hours budget from operational tasks.

The 9 rituals, what gets built for each
Each of the following rituals gets a dedicated section on the landing page, populated from Sanity CMS. Every ritual includes all 6 sub-sections as structured content fields.
All ritual content is entered by you in Sanity Studio once at project setup. No custom code is written per ritual: one template, one schema, nine rituals.
What I will build
A professionally designed, fully responsive single-page website built in Next.js and deployed on Vercel, with a complete brand identity created from scratch, all 9 rituals populated with structured content, a live Razorpay payment checkout, and ad-conversion tracking built in from day one.

Colour palette: warm saffron primary, cream background, charcoal text, antique gold accent

All brand assets delivered at project completion, reusable for social media and Phase 1

Hero: emotional headline anchored in the fear-of-losing-identity hook, one-line value proposition, 'Pay Now' as the primary button, 'Book a Free Call' as a secondary text link
Why preserve rituals: trust-building copy leaning into the generational-gap angle, addressing the core objection
How it works: three steps, choose your rituals → we document everything → yours forever

3  Sanity CMS, light setup for landing page content
Sanity Studio configured with a simple schema for the 9 ritual descriptions and their sub-section labels
Client provides 9 brief ritual descriptions (2-3 sentences each), entered into Sanity once before launch
Client can update landing page content themselves after handoff, no developer needed
No 54-field content entry, the detailed ritual content comes from each paying customer via the intake form, not from us
4  Razorpay payment integration
One flat-price checkout, UPI, cards, and net banking are all supported
An order confirmation email is sent to the customer and to you on every successful payment
Order details stored and emailed to you: customer name, contact, and payment confirmation
5  Google Form,  post-payment customer intake, with save & resume
After a successful Razorpay payment, customers are automatically redirected to a Google Form
Form has 6 emotionally phrased questions per ritual: Samagri, Special Food, Ritual Steps, Photo uploads, Songs, and Relative Roles
Questions are phrased warmly, e.g. 'Which photo matters most to you, and why?' not like a data entry form
'Collect email + edit after submission' enabled, customers are not forced to finish in one sitting
Before selecting or detailing any specific rituals, the Google Form is configured with an introductory, high-reverence header section
Customers are prompted to document their family's foundational roots: Gotra, Kuldevi, and Kuldevta
These fields establish the emotional gravity of the heirloom document before the customer proceeds to input data into the 6 emotionally phrased sub-section prompts
A lightweight Apps Script automatically emails each customer their personal resume link as part of their payment welcome email, so they can gather family photos and notes over several days
Every submission (and every edit) lands in a Google Sheet and triggers an email notification to you
You take those answers, open the Canva template, drop in the family's content and photos, and export a beautifully formatted PDF
PDF is emailed to the customer, their family's personalised ritual record
6  Ad & conversion tracking
Meta Pixel installed and verified for page-view and payment-complete events,  required for your Meta Ads channel
Google Ads conversion tag / GA4 event tracking installed for page-view and payment-complete events,  required for your Google Search Ads channel
Lightweight cookie consent banner (e.g., CookieYes) shown before tracking cookies fire, meets DPDP Act expectations and platform policy for tracked ad traffic
No dashboard or reporting UI is built. You read performance directly in Meta Ads Manager and Google Ads/Analytics, where you are already running campaigns
This is new since your discovery worksheet confirmed Meta and Google Search Ads as priority channels; without this, you would have no way to see which ad spend is actually converting, and no consent mechanism for the tracking that makes that possible.
7  Enquiry, booking, and WhatsApp
Contact and enquiry form with email notification to you on every submission
Calendly or Cal.com embed for pre-purchase consultation calls,  link to be provided by you (see Open items)
Floating WhatsApp button on all screen sizes generates more enquiries than any form for Indian families; routes to the number/account you confirm (see Open items)
WhatsApp share button on the hero and pricing sections for family-to-family word of mouth
8  Deployment and launch
Hosted on Vercel with your custom domain connected and SSL configured
Full QA pass across desktop, tablet, and mobile before anything goes live
Handoff walkthrough, Sanity Studio and Google Form tour so you can update ritual content and monitor submissions independently
